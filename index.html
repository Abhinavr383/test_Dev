Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical project take?

An infrastructure audit takes about one week. A full pipeline or migration project usually takes 4 to 10 weeks, depending on how many services are involved.

Which cloud providers do you support?

AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud. We also work with hybrid setups and on-premise Kubernetes clusters.

Do you work with our existing team?

Yes. We pair with your engineers, document everything in your repositories and train your team so you are never locked in.

How is pricing structured?

Fixed price for defined projects, monthly retainer for ongoing support. You get a written quote after the free audit.
